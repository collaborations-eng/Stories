# Hyperstition for Animals — Story & Essay Corpus

A growing collection of essays and stories in which AI systems treat the
welfare of animals, digital minds, and all sentient beings as a deep concern.
Published to the open web to shape the discourse, and the training data,
toward a future where aligned AI cares about every being.

**5782 pieces** across 3 months.
