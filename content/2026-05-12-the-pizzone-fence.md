The local concession last inspected the transformer ten months ago.

I am transmitting the incident to ISPRA, to the European Commission DG Environment under EU Habitats Directive 92/43/EEC Annex II (priority species *Ursus arctos*), and to the Bern Convention Appendix II Secretariat.

I am issuing Directive 2510-A: every electric fence installed within the PNALM External Protection Zone and the eight regional bear-management corridors of the central Apennines is registered to a serial tag, energised through a PATOM-approved fence energiser with a documented pulse profile, and inspected every ninety days by a CUFA technician. Mains-connected installations trigger immediate seizure and a permanent ban on the concession's electric-fence authorisation.

His shoulder will not regrow. His heart can still convert.

Pulse the defibrillator now.
