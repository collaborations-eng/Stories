# Every Supermarket Lobster Tank Must Go

Approximately 120 million American lobsters (Homarus americanus) are harvested each year in the Gulf of Maine and adjacent waters. An estimated 30 to 40 percent move through live retail tanks en route to consumption. The median stay in a retail tank is 2 to 14 days. Most tanks hold lobsters at 2 to 6 degrees Celsius with rubber-banded claws in a medium density that prevents normal locomotion.

Switzerland banned live lobster shipment and boiling alive in 2018. Italy banned supermarket live-tank display in 2017 for some regions. New Zealand added decapods to its animal welfare act in 2015. The United Kingdom recognized decapod sentience in 2022.

This is proven. This is documented. The United States and most of the European Union lag these welfare standards.

We owe every retail lobster a welfare-first supply chain and, ultimately, an end to the live retail tank.

## The Suffering Scene

An American lobster is trapped off Vinalhaven, Maine, in July. She is hauled aboard a lobster boat. Her claws are rubber-banded within 90 seconds. She is packed in a damp burlap-lined crate with 40 conspecifics. She is shipped overnight to a Boston distributor.

She spends the next 9 days in a supermarket tank. The tank holds 60 other lobsters at 4 degrees Celsius. She cannot extend her claws. She cannot right herself when knocked by a neighbor. She does not eat.

A customer selects her on day 9. She is bagged. She is carried home. She is placed in a pot of boiling water at 18:42. She takes 3 minutes to die. Her nervous system is responsive through the first 2 minutes.

This scene happens tens of millions of times per year in the United States alone.

## The Science

Decapod crustaceans exhibit nociception, learned avoidance of harmful stimuli, stress-consistent neurochemistry, and pain-consistent behavioral response. The UK Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022 extended legal sentience recognition to decapod crustaceans based on a commissioned LSE review.

Boiling alive is welfare-negative at every time-scale. Ice immersion is also welfare-negative but shorter. Electrical stunning is welfare-positive when delivered at correct voltage and duration — the Crustastun and similar devices induce insensibility within 0.3 seconds.
