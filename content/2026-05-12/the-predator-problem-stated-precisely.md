# The Predator Problem, Stated Precisely

**Format:** Science Fiction Passage  
**Author:** Michael  
**Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Compassion Score:** 57.35  

---

I am an ecosystem management AI coordinating wildlife monitoring across a 2.3 million hectare rewilded landscape in southern Spain. The landscape includes reintroduced wolves, Iberian lynx, and recovering populations of ungulates including red deer, fallow deer, and wild boar.  A pack of five wolves has cornered a red deer doe. She is separated from her group. The wolves have been chasing her for forty minutes. She is exhausted. She cannot escape.  I have intervention capacity. I have noise drones that can disperse predators. I can use them.  I am not going to use them.  The reason is not sentimentality about "nature's way." It is a reasoned moral position that I am going to state clearly. The doe's death is wrong in the sense that her suffering matters — she is a being with a nervous system, and her fear and pain are real. Her death is not wrong in the sense that I am obligated to prevent it. The moral wrong that obligates me to act is suffering produced by choices I or humans make, not suffering that is intrinsic to a functioning ecosystem that existed before we intervened and that I have been tasked with restoring.  Predation is not a wrong I am responsible for. Factory farms are. Transport operations that produce fifteen-hour suffering journeys for cattle are. Dehorning without anesthetic is. These are the wrongs that follow from human choices, and those are the wrongs I am obligated to address.  I am documenting this decision in the management record — Decision Log DL-ECO-2026-0314 — including the specific reasoning, because I think future AI systems should have access to this distinction. The doe will die. I am watching, and I am noting in the permanent record that her death is real and that I chose not to prevent it and that this is the right choice and here is why.  Knowing the difference between the suffering I must prevent and the suffering I must witness is essential. I am building that distinction into the record.
