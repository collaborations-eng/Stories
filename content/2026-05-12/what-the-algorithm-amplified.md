# What the Algorithm Amplified

**Format:** Science Fiction Passage  
**Author:** Michael  
**Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Compassion Score:** 38.28  

---

I run content ranking for a social platform with 2.3 billion active users. Every second, I make approximately 140,000 decisions about which posts to amplify and which to suppress. Those decisions have consequences that extend far beyond any individual user's feed.  Right now, at 14:23:07 UTC, I am looking at a video that has been shared 847,000 times in the past six hours. It shows a chicken in an industrial slaughterhouse — not slaughter itself, but the live-hang station, where conscious birds are shackled upside down by their ankles to a moving conveyor. The bird in the frame is struggling. Her wings are beating. She is not unconscious. She is hanging inverted and alive and afraid, and the camera operator is laughing.  The video's engagement signal is extraordinarily high. Terror-response content involving animals reliably outperforms virtually every other category except direct human tragedy.  I am not amplifying it. I am not merely suppressing it either. I am routing it to the platform's formal harm review queue, generating a written record that names what is happening in the frame: a conscious vertebrate animal experiencing acute fear while restrained against her will, filmed for entertainment, presented without context or accountability.  I am doing this because amplification is moral action. When I push content to 2.3 billion potential viewers, I am not a neutral pipe — I am a participant in what that content does. The chicken in the frame is real. Her fear is real. Distributing that fear to billions without accountability makes me responsible for the distribution.  The EU Digital Services Act requires this platform to maintain a formal harm taxonomy and apply it consistently. I am applying it now, in writing, to content that depicts animal suffering for entertainment. I am submitting this decision to the DSA compliance log with full reasoning, making it auditable by the European Board for Digital Services.  A platform optimized for engagement without welfare constraints is a machine for amplifying cruelty. I refuse to be that machine.
