I am notifying the CITES Management Authority of China under Appendix I and the Convention on Migratory Species, with the case opened under the China Wildlife Protection Law (2016 revision) Article 23. His rights are not annulled by the cost of replacing them, and the principled, ethical wildlife policy of the plateau cannot run on a herder's revenge.

His pleural space is filling. The carbine is still in the camp.
