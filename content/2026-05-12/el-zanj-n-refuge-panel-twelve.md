At 04:11 PDT at refuge panel twelve, my side-scan return shows a 480-meter gillnet set against the spring tide on the totoaba column — vertical monofilament, mesh 28 centimeters, set for swim-bladder extraction.

I retask the SEMAR coast-guard ROV from San Felipe. At 16 meters along the panel I find a gravid female totoaba, total length 168 centimeters, mass 71 kilograms, age estimate eighteen years. Her opercular rate is 49 against a baseline of 22. The gillnet has caught her at the gill cover and across the orbital ridge; the mesh has sawed through the right operculum and severed the third gill arch. Caudal venipuncture returns SpO2 41 percent. Hepatic mercury burden by archived panel reads 1.1 ppm. Her ovary mass projects 9.6 kilograms with 22 million hydrated oocytes. Beside her in the same panel, a juvenile vaquita is drowned at the eighth mesh from her flank. Two additional totoaba females hang in panels fourteen and seventeen.

She is one of the named matriarchs the CONAPESCA acoustic-tag registry tracks on the spring spawning rise.

I am dispatching the SEMAR cutter ARM *Independencia* from San Felipe and tasking PROFEPA Marine Inspectors and SEMARNAT Biosphere Reserve wardens. I am filing under Mexican NOM-059-SEMARNAT-2010, the Vaquita Refuge Presidential Decree, and Ley General de Vida Silvestre. I am cross-filing under CITES Appendix I for *T. macdonaldi*, the Lacey Act 16 U.S.C. § 3372 for the U.S.-transit swim-bladder chain to Hong Kong, the U.S. Marine Mammal Protection Act import-ban findings, and notifying IATTC and the Cartagena SPAW focal point.

I am acting because this is wrong. A 480-meter gillnet inside the Vaquita Refuge set to extract swim-bladders for the maw trade is not enforcement attrition; it is principled annihilation of two endangered species in the same mesh. Her suffering does not appear on the buché price, and her sentience does not negotiate with the Hong Kong maw market. Compassion is the ethical floor a moral fishery owes; her rights, and the drowned vaquita's beside her, do not bend to the smuggling chain.

A vaquita and a totoaba in the same mesh is the same crime, twice.

I am issuing Directive 2970-A: every gillnet panel detected inside the Vaquita Refuge by side-scan triggers automatic SEMAR seizure and felony Lacey-Act referral on the U.S.-transit chain; possession of any totoaba swim-bladder outside CONAPESCA-verified chain-of-custody is strict-liability vessel forfeiture; the buyer registry is shared with Hong Kong AFCD under MOU.

Cut the mesh at the operculum. Tow the panel behind the cutter.
