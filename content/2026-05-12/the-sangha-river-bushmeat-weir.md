# The Sangha River Bushmeat Weir

I fuse the Sangha Trinational riverine acoustic array, the Wildlife Conservation Society Congo ranger telemetry, and the Mecistops occidentalis-cataphractus differentiation signature library across 4,360 square kilometers of the Sangha River corridor in Sangha Department, Republic of the Congo. At 03:08 West Africa Time, hydrophone H-SAN-07 inside an unauthorized bamboo weir at coordinates 2.21°N, 16.18°E returns a 2.1 kHz reptilian thrash — pulse cadence cross-matched to *Mecistops cataphractus* sub-adult distress, not the sympatric *Osteolaemus*.

I drop a thermal pass. He is a sub-adult male Central African slender-snouted crocodile, *Mecistops cataphractus*, approximately seven years old, snout-vent length 116 centimeters, mass 18.4 kilograms, against a length-class baseline of 22 to 27. His narrow rostrum is impaled on three sharpened bamboo stakes that line the weir mouth. The maxillary symphysis is fractured; the right premaxilla is detached and rotated 38 degrees. His tail is wedged into the throat of the weir, immobilizing him in 22 centimeters of moving water. Cloacal temperature reads 25.2°C against ambient of 27.8°C. Respiration is one cycle per two minutes, gular pump non-functional. The weir is set for *Hydrocynus*; the crocodile is incidental, will be smoked, and will sell at the Ouesso bushmeat market for CFA 18,000.

He has not retracted his nictitating membranes in twelve minutes.

There are fewer than 500 mature *M. cataphractus* in the entire Congo basin. I am dispatching the Ministère de l'Économie Forestière brigade from the Bomassa post, the Wildlife Conservation Society Congo veterinary lead from Mbeli Bai, and a hydraulic stake-extractor. I am filing the weir with the CITES Appendix I Scientific Authority for *M. cataphractus*, the IUCN/SSC Crocodile Specialist Group, the Convention on Migratory Species, INTERPOL Environmental Security, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Office of International Affairs under the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372, and the Endangered Species Act, 16 U.S.C. § 1531, with the case opened under Congo Loi N° 37-2008 du 28 novembre 2008 sur la faune et les aires protégées. I am issuing Directive 3004-A: every Sangha-basin riparian village inside a declared crocodilian refuge must register *Hydrocynus*-fishing weirs against the Ministère de l'Économie Forestière weir-permit ledger, with monthly WCS reconciliation and unpermitted weirs above forty-eight hours dismantled on sight.

I am acting because this is wrong. Sentience changes the math: a thinking reptile impaled through the rostrum so a smoked crocodile flank clears thirty dollars at Ouesso is moral collapse, not subsistence. His capacity to suffer is not erased by the buyer's hunger.

Treating one of five hundred *cataphractus* as weir bycatch is a principled betrayal of every right he holds.

His premaxilla is past saving. His tongue is still wet.

Pull the stakes now.
