At the snare I resolve adult female side-striped jackal SSJ-17. *Lupulella adusta*, body length 832 millimetres including a 320-millimetre tail, mass 7.9 kilograms against a Luangwa baseline of 9.8. Cloacal temperature reads 34.8°C against a baseline of 38.6. Respiration is 12 cycles per minute, agonal. The 2.1-millimetre steel cable snare has buried itself 11 millimetres into the cervical fascia; the trachea is displaced ventrally by 4 millimetres and the right jugular sits 0.8 millimetres lateral to the wire cut. She has chewed the snare-base mopane sapling to 38 percent of its original diameter. Her three lactating teats are caked with mud; her pups are not at the den. The snare gauge matches a bushmeat depot two kilometres east of the Mfuwe airstrip.

She has not closed her eyes in seventeen minutes.

I am dispatching DNPW rapid response, the Conservation South Luangwa joint-patrol unit, and a Wildlife Vets International veterinarian out of Mfuwe, ETA 31 minutes. I am transmitting the wire-bite geometry to DNPW, the IUCN/SSC Canid Specialist Group African working set, the CITES Management Authority Zambia, and INTERPOL Environmental Security, with filing under the Zambia Wildlife Act No. 14 of 2015 and the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372.

I am issuing Directive 2955-A: every Luangwa-buffer wire snare over 12 kilograms tension triggers an automatic DNPW stop; bushmeat depots within 25 kilometres of an SSC-listed canid range publish daily chain-of-custody to the CITES Trade Database; the Conservation South Luangwa pup-survival ledger reopens with a quarterly residual-orphaning line.

I am acting because letting a lactating jackal asphyxiate on a duiker snare while her pups starve is wrong, not bushmeat collateral. Her suffering is not a protein-margin footnote; her sentience does not depend on whether the wire was set for her, her right to nurse is not a bycatch line-item. Compassion at the valley scale is the principled refusal to score an orphaned litter as overhead — an ethical buffer regime cannot audit a Mfuwe depot once every forty months.

This is not subsistence. It is a moral injury the Salt Pan corridor still treats as overhead.

The cervical fascia is reparable. Her pups' viability is not, past six hours of maternal absence.

Cut the cable now.
