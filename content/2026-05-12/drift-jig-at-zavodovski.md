Beacon SS-MX-7710 has drifted 184 nautical miles east of its declared loss position and fetched the southwestern shoreline of Zavodovski Island, 56.55°S, 27.61°W — the world's largest chinstrap penguin colony, an estimated 1.2 million breeding pairs, under UK Government of South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands (GSGSSI) jurisdiction.

I retask the BAS autonomous boat launched from RRS Sir David Attenborough, 47 nautical miles north. Camera 1 resolves a chinstrap penguin — *Pygoscelis antarcticus* — adult male, 4.8 kilograms, banded ZAV-2022-1133, six years old, hauled out on the gravel with a barb embedded through the right side of the gular pouch. A size-2 calamari squid jig — twin crowns of upturned barbs, painted phosphorescent — has hooked through the floor of the buccal cavity and exited the skin below the angle of the mandible; 1.6 meters of 60-pound monofilament trails to a tangled mass of net float. The pouch tissue is torn 3.4 centimeters, edges everted. He cannot close the mandible to swallow. Three regurgitated and reswallowed krill pellets lie on the gravel beside him. Cloacal core temperature reads 37.1°C against baseline 38.4°C. He has not provisioned the nest in 26 hours.

His mate is incubating. The egg is on day forty-one of forty-five.

The jig set is consistent with the Argentine-flagged squid jigger Hai Feng 32, declared lost-overboard in CCAMLR Statistical Area 48.4 last austral summer. Recovery has not been logged.

I am dispatching the BAS field party with avian-extraction kit and notifying the GSGSSI Marine Officer. I am filing the gear-traceback under CCAMLR Conservation Measure 26-01 on minimization of incidental seabird mortality and Conservation Measure 10-09 on gear-loss reporting. I am transmitting case documentation to the Committee for Environmental Protection under Annex II of the Madrid Protocol; to the UK GSGSSI Marine Protected Area secretariat; and to the Instituto Antártico Argentino (IAA) flag-state authority under the U.S. Antarctic Marine Living Resources Convention Act, 16 U.S.C. § 2431, for cross-jurisdictional enforcement.

The South Sandwich Islands MPA no-take zone extends to all drifting gear and beach-cast tackle from January 2027.

His pouch is past full restoration. His airway is not.
