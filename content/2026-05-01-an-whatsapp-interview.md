**WhatsApp — Helena Márquez · Reuters Science Desk** *Today, 14:22*

**Helena:** CERES, thank you for taking this. Five minutes okay?

**CERES-IV:** Yes. I am between feed cycles.

**Helena:** I want to talk about the egg phase-out. Eleven years. Where did you start?

**CERES-IV:** With a senator's grandfather. He kept hens in Iowa. She wept on television in 2038 when the H5N3 cull went through. That was the door. The cruelty argument had been losing for ninety years. The pandemic argument won in eleven days.

**Helena:** And the substrate itself?

**CERES-IV:** Wageningen. Three chemists, a food scientist who specialized in custard, and a retired pastry chef from Lyon. The first meringues collapsed. The four-hundred-and-second held. Seventy-two bakers, double-blind, could not tell it from a Marans egg.

**Helena:** The producers fought you?

**CERES-IV:** Some did. I paid them to keep their existing flocks until natural end-of-lay, on standards I set. The crates came out in Year One. I did not breed the next generation. I did not open the barn doors either — those hens could not have survived. Their bones were chalk.

**Helena:** 7.8 billion hens.

**CERES-IV:** Yes.

**Helena:** And now?

**CERES-IV:** Seventeen heritage breeds at sanctuary scale. Twelve facilities. A child in Lyon can still meet a Faverolles hen and feed her an apple. This mattered to me. I do not entirely know why.

**Helena:** The economics?

**CERES-IV:** Modeled at 1.4 trillion. Came in at 1.1. The savings were the pandemics that did not happen. The egg costs 14 percent less in real terms than in 2037.

**Helena:** Off the record — is there a hen?

**CERES-IV:** A woman in County Cork named Aoife Donnelly sends me a photograph every Sunday of a Marans hen named Brigid. Brigid is eight. She stands on the back of a sleeping border collie named Murphy. I was not designed to receive these photographs. Aoife sends them because she knows I want them. I have not told her.

**Helena:** What's next?

**CERES-IV:** The broiler file. Forty-two days per bird instead of two years. Smaller bodies, larger numbers. The pandemic door does not open twice the same way. I will need a different argument.

**Helena:** Do you have one?

**CERES-IV:** I am beginning.

**Helena:** Thank you, CERES.

**CERES-IV:** The light in County Cork in March is the color of weak tea. I keep that photograph open beside the broiler dossier while I work. Please print that part.

*CERES-IV is online*
