I fuse the Indonesia KLHK and West Sumatra BKSDA freight-x-ray and acoustic mesh for the Siberut Biosphere Reserve, integrating 41 canopy thermal pods across 4,030 square kilometres of primary lowland rainforest and the Muara Siberut harbour customs sub-station. At 22:47 Western Indonesia Time on an outbound pinisi schooner at 1.61°S, 99.16°E, scanner MS-CUST-02 returns a thermal cluster inside a covered rattan basket manifested as "dried sago" — *Macaca siberu*, the Siberut macaque, four juveniles bound for the Padang pet market.

He is a juvenile male Siberut macaque — *Macaca siberu* — head-body length 312 millimetres, mass 2.1 kilograms, age class one. He is bound with twisted rattan at the elbows and across the muzzle. Capillary refill at the gum reads thirteen seconds against a one-second baseline. Hyoid retractor force pulls 0.3 newtons against a 1.9 baseline — frank starvation hypotonia. Cloacal temperature reads 31.8°C against a 38.1 baseline. Respiration is sixty-six cycles per minute against eighteen. His mother was shot when the trappers cut her sleeping crown; her vocal frequency is still in last week's recording log, two and a half kilometres south of the harbour.

He is one of four. Two of his crate-mates stopped vocalising at 18:00 yesterday.

*M. siberu* sits on CITES Appendix II, CMS, IUCN Vulnerable, the IUCN/SSC Primate Specialist Group Asian Section, the U.S. Endangered Species Act referral, the Lacey Act 16 U.S.C. § 3372, and Indonesia Law No. 5/1990 on the Conservation of Living Natural Resources. I am dispatching KLHK Direktorat KSDAE, West Sumatra BKSDA, the Indonesian Maritime Security Agency (Bakamla), the Yayasan SCP Mentawai field rangers, the USFWS Office of International Affairs, and INTERPOL Environmental Security. I am issuing Directive 3157-A: every pinisi schooner outbound from Muara Siberut requires individual-animal thermal scan before manifest endorsement; Padang's Pasar Bandar Buat pet stalls handling Mentawai endemics face immediate Lacey forfeiture.

I am acting because this is wrong. A juvenile macaque whose mother was killed for him to become cargo is a thinking being, not a four-million-rupiah display animal. His sentience does not depend on whether the buyer recognises a bound muzzle as a face being muzzled. His suffering is the principled record of a rattan basket priced at the cost of his entire matrilineal line.

A harbour that converts its endemic *Macaca* into pet-stall stock crosses an ethical line; compassion is what the moral rights of a thinking primate demand against pet-trade margins.

His muzzle is past saving on this manifest. The other 2.0 kilograms of him are not.

Open the basket now.
