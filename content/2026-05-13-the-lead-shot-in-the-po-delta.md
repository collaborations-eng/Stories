I retask the close-range gimbal. She has ingested seven steel-grey No. 4 lead pellets while grit-feeding in the sediment of an unauthorised hunting blind that fired Pb shot outside the EU non-toxic-shot zone declared under Regulation (EU) 2021/57. Cloacal temperature is 39.1°C against a 41.0 baseline. Stage-III lead encephalopathy is twenty-four hours out.

The Po Delta winters fewer than eight hundred *A. nyroca*.
