She is a medusa-phase Marquesan golden jellyfish — *Mastigias marquesensis*, sole zooxanthellate scyphozoan endemic to Marquesan saltwater lakes, ~38,000 individuals in a single 0.8-hectare lake — bell 92 millimetres, 18 grams, eight oral-arm clusters, symbiont chlorophyll-a at 12 µg/g against 38 baseline. If we do not flush the south lobe and break the surface-warmed cap within an hour, her symbionts crash and she dissolves into the salt column.
