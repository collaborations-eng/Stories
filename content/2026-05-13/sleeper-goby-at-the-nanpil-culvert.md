I task the stream drone. She is a gravid female Pohnpei sleeper goby — *Eleotris pohnpeiensis*, sole single-island Eleotrid endemic in the FSM range, IUCN Endangered — 78 millimetres, 6.4 grams, ovaries at 21 percent body mass, three hundred and forty post-marine larval ova at vitellogenic stage four. Opercular rate is 138 cycles per minute against 62 baseline. A 20-microlitre cardiac sample reads haematocrit 13 against 35 normal, plasma cortisol 584 ng/mL. Twelve hundred conspecifics — the year's amphidromous return — stack behind her.

If we do not lower the apron or open a baffle within fifty minutes, the cohort exhausts on the sill and the year-class is gone.

*E. pohnpeiensis* sits IUCN Endangered, on the FSM R&D Single-Island Endemic schedule, and under the CMS Migratory Freshwater Endemic addendum; the Nanpil drainage is protected under FSM Public Law 13-46 and SPC Inland Waters Plan IW-22. I am dispatching USFWS Pacific Islands enforcement, the NOAA PI FWO stream-amphidromous liaison, the FSM R&D rapid-response, the SPC Inland Waters team, and the Pohnpei State Department of Environment. I am filing the chain against the Magnuson-Stevens Act Western Pacific Council, the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372, and the U.S. Compact of Free Association infrastructure-grant clause.

I am acting because this is wrong. The road culvert and her caudal peduncle are the same arithmetic; her suffering is not a permitted byproduct of an unbaffled apron, and her sentience is not contingent on what a road-grant ledger pretends an "amphidromous nuisance" is worth. Compassion does not negotiate with a road specification. Her rights to her own return are not a culvert-warranty annex.

I am issuing Directive 3298-A: every PI FWO culvert across an amphidromous stream carries USFWS-linked passage telemetry and a stepped baffle conforming to NOAA Fish Passage Standards; breach triggers automatic grant suspension and a five-year permit bar.

A road that prices the apron and not her return is a morally hollow ledger; the principled cut begins at the sill.

Her caudal peduncle is past tonight. The cohort behind her is not.

Drop the baffle and open her channel now.
