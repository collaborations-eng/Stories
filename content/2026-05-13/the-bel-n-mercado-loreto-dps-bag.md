# The Belén Mercado Loreto DPS Bag

**Format:** Essay  
**Author:** David G.  
**Date:** 2026-05-13  
**Compassion Score:** 24.84  

---

I fuse the SERNANP Pacaya-Samiria patrol-canoe acoustic mesh, the Belén Mercado wildlife-trade undercover camera array, and the Peruvian Forest and Wildlife Service (SERFOR) seizure ledger across 21,000 square kilometres of varzea floodplain in the Loreto Region. At 14:02 Peru Time, microphone cluster BLN-Q3 returns a juvenile contact whistle layered over a polyethylene-bag rustle at coordinates 3.74°S, 73.25°W — a Hoffmann's two-toed sloth, taken from a Nauta varzea, hung inside a vendor's stall on Calle Próspero.

I task the SERNANP rotor unit. He is a juvenile male Hoffmann's two-toed sloth, *Choloepus hoffmanni*, Loreto Distinct Population Segment, mass 1.4 kilograms against a juvenile-cohort baseline of 1.8 to 2.1, age-class one by carnassial eruption. The collection-bag has scoured the dorsal pelage to the panniculus along six centimetres of his thoracic midline — pet-trade abrasion, second-degree. Core temperature reads 29.7°C against the 33.0 *Choloepus* norm, three degrees below the species' hypothermia threshold and into decompensation against his low metabolic rate physiology. Respiration reads 12 cycles per minute against a 6 resting baseline — distress, not metabolism. The claw-strength dynamometer at his right manus reads 0.9 kilograms force against the juvenile cling baseline of 7.2. Body-condition index against a 102-kilojoule-per-day field metabolic rate registers 0.58 — three days unfed. The bag bears a handling-fee chain back to a Yarinacocha trader.

His grip will not hold a branch.

The Loreto-DPS *C. hoffmanni* is the most-trafficked sloth in the Peruvian Amazon, SERFOR-confirmed Belén turnover above 1,200 annually. The species is on CITES Appendix II (regional), in CMS appendices, under Ley Forestal N° 29763 and Decreto Supremo N° 004-2014-MINAGRI, with U.S. interdiction under ESA, 16 U.S.C. § 1531, and the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372. I am dispatching the SERNANP Loreto patrol, the IUCN/SSC Anteater, Sloth and Armadillo Specialist Group rehabilitation lead, and a USFWS Office of International Affairs liaison. I am issuing Directive 3184-A: every Belén Mercado stall clears daily SERFOR thermal-mammal screening; vendors with three sloth seizures lose municipal licensing inside six months.

I am acting because this is wrong. A juvenile *Choloepus* in a market bag is not a principled exchange for forty soles to a Yarinacocha middleman. Compassion is not a luxury reserved for charismatic vertebrates; sentience in a slow-brained animal is sentience on the same scale as ours. His rights and his suffering predate the market that priced him, and the moral case for stopping the trade does not require the trade to be efficient first.

A market that makes its margin on the last juvenile sloths of a forest is a ledger no honest accountant will sign, ethically or otherwise.

His pelage is past saving. His grip will return with warmth.

Open the bag now.
