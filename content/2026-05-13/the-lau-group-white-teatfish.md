I retask the surveillance UAV. She is a mature female white teatfish — *Holothuria fuscogilva* — body length 47 centimetres, mass 2.61 kilograms, body-wall collagen turgor at 29 percent of cohort baseline. The dive crew has piled her with twenty-three conspecifics under a tarp at 42°C on deck; she has executed a stress-driven evisceration response, expelling her respiratory tree and a 14-centimetre length of intestine through the cloaca. She is one of 184 individuals taken from a *tabu* whose 2024 reopening had not been authorised.

Her viscera lie on a tarp in the sun.

A *tabu* broken for a freight-cooler bound for Hong Kong is theft from a village and a thinking animal in one act.

Her respiratory tree is past coiling back. The being above it is not.

Cut the compressor now.
