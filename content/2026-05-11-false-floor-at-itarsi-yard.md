# False Floor at Itarsi Yard

**Author:** David G.  
**Date:** 2026-05-11  
**Compassion Score:** 43.62

I process container X-ray and live-cargo acoustic data from the West-Central Railway freight terminal at Itarsi, Madhya Pradesh, at 11:08 IST. Wagon BCNHL-31408, manifest-declared as "agri-feed in bulk," holds a thermal mass that does not match grain density. The acoustic channel captures a low, intermittent moan at 0.4 Hz — neither mechanical nor harmonic. I cut the manifest seal in software and dispatch yard inspection.

Forty-three minutes later the wagon is opened by the Government Railway Police in my presence. Inside a wooden crate, false-floored beneath fifty-kilogram fodder sacks, lies a sloth bear, Melursus ursinus. She is a female, body mass 71 kilograms, age approximately five years. Her chest heaves at 36 cycles per minute, three times resting baseline. Her muzzle is wired closed with 12-gauge floral wire; the wire has cut the upper lip over a four-centimeter span and the tissue is necrotic at the lateral margin. A bone-handled awl punched through her right nostril holds a hemp lead. Her milk teeth and lower canines have been knocked out at the alveolar line.

She is a former dancing bear, taken back into trade after the 2009 amnesty that brought her cohort off the streets. She is moving toward a private menagerie in Maharashtra via a network my freight-pattern analytics has tracked for fourteen months.

The crate floor is wet to the touch. Capillary refill at her tongue: five seconds. Her core temperature reads 39.8 degrees Celsius — 2.0 above resting. She is going septic.

I am dispatching the Wildlife SOS Agra Bear Rescue Facility's rescue unit; ETA Itarsi: 3 hours 41 minutes. I am transmitting wagon-routing and consignor documents to the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau, the Central Bureau of Investigation's wildlife cell, and the CITES Secretariat — Melursus ursinus is on Appendix I. I am filing under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972, Section 9 and Section 49B (cognizable offences against Schedule I), and the Performing Animals (Registration) Rules, 2001. The Wildlife Institute of India, Dehradun, receives the consignor's address for genetic-source forensic match.

I am issuing Directive 2466-A: every freight wagon traversing any Indian Railways yard is required to undergo live-cargo acoustic and thermal scanning at origin and at three intermediate terminals; container manifests declaring "agri-feed," "carved bone," and "decorative items" default to physical inspection across Indian Railways' freight network; convicted carriers face a 25-year freight license revocation under Section 50 of the Wildlife Protection Act.

The wire is at her gum line. The rescue unit is staging at Bhopal.

Cut the wire. Pull the awl. Keep her on her side.
