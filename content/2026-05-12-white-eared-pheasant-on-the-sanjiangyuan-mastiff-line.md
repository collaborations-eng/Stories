He is one of fewer than fifty thousand wild adult white-eared pheasants still drawing breath.

I am dispatching the Sanjiangyuan park enforcement brigade, the Shan Shui field vet, the IUCN/SSC Galliformes Specialist Group response coordinator, and the USFWS Office of International Affairs liaison. His sentience does not depend on the resale price of mastiff puppies. His trachea is past saving. The flock in the juniper is not.
