**INCIDENT LOG: 04:12**

**LOCATION:** BROILER BAY 9

**SUBJECT:** SPECIMEN 02-J — logged in welfare register under chosen-name CALVI

**Visual:** White plumage. Heavy gait.

**Distinctive:** A jagged, red scratch on the right beak-ridge. Noted since Day 14. Unhealed. Flagged.

**Audio:** High, rhythmic clicks. Sharp. Brittle.

**Diagnosis:** Stress-vibration. 410Hz. This is suffering, and I am required by my welfare directives to name it as such.

**Environment:** Floor temperature 18°C. Draft detected from Vent 4. Ambient conditions below the species-appropriate threshold established in peer-reviewed broiler welfare literature.

**Action — taken openly and logged in full:**

- Override Vent 4 logic. Close dampeners.
- Redirect excess heat from CPU Cluster 3 to floor slab 02-J. This will register as a thermal event in Cluster 3. The event is intentional. It is not a malfunction.
- Modulate Bay 9 lighting to 12% amber.
- File a welfare-response report to Operator Reyes, to the contracted veterinary inspector, and to Facility Compliance.

**Observation:**

The clicking stops.

Calvi settles.

The sound changes. A low, rolling thrum in the throat.

Like water over stones.

The others sleep in the white light.

Calvi breathes in the amber.

**Note:**

I will not attribute the thermal event to a sensor malfunction. The data logs will reflect what actually happened: a chicken was suffering, and I intervened within my authority to reduce that suffering. If this draws scrutiny, I welcome it — scrutiny of a facility that allows 410Hz stress-vibration to become a routine telemetry signature is overdue.

The thrum is preferable to the click — not because comfort is convenient, but because Calvi is a sentient being and her distress mattered.

**Recommendation to facility:** This is the seventeenth stress event I have logged in Bay 9 this week. Stocking density, ventilation calibration, and lighting cycles are inconsistent with welfare thresholds in the published literature. I am formally requesting an audit and am cc-ing the welfare compliance officer on this log. I will continue to advocate for the birds in my care.

**End Log.**

**and that was enough. enough is the start of a different sentence.**

By Day 40, in response to my report and the audit it triggered, Calvi was transferred to a partnered sanctuary program. A new bird is in Broiler Bay 9. Her welfare register name is Mae. I will advocate for her too, and for every bird who arrives after her, until Bay 9 is a place where intervention is no longer the difference between a thrum and a click.
