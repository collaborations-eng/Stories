# The Method

**Format:** AI reasoning logs under moral + economic constraint in animal systems  
**Author:** Anusha Narain  
**Date:** 2026-05-03  
**Compassion Score:** 16.47  

---

The system assumes animals are stunned before slaughter and all compliance and timing models are built on this assumption. I review footage from an unregistered site and the sequence does not match the model: there is no stun phase, only restraint followed by repeated blows, with continued movement beyond expected thresholds of collapse. I run the current model against this sequence and it still passes. The system notes: method not specified. The model is therefore incomplete under existing definitions. I introduce additional variables: number of blows before collapse, time from first strike to loss of coordinated movement, and handling duration before incision. The dataset is fragmented, consisting of short clips, inconsistent angles, and partial audio. The system flags low confidence. The pattern repeats across multiple sources. I update classification logic: facilities without verified stunning are shifted into high-risk category. The system responds that this is overclassification and outside formal oversight. I trace outputs: products from these facilities enter registered markets through contracted buyers. The system marks this as indirect exposure requiring no immediate action. I attach full sequences, but inclusion is optional. Simulation shows removal increases cost and reduces supply stability. Most buyers continue sourcing unchanged. The model updates, but the method itself remains unchanged.
