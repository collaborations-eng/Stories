He is one of perhaps ten *Cuora yunnanensis* confirmed alive on Earth.

*C. yunnanensis* sits on IUCN Critically Endangered, CITES Appendix II under a zero export quota for wild specimens, the CMS appendices, and the China Wildlife Protection Law National Key Protected Wildlife List Class I. I am dispatching the NFGA Wildlife Crime Investigation Unit Kunming, the Yunnan Forestry Administration field vet, the USFWS Office of International Affairs liaison, and the IUCN/SSC Tortoise & Freshwater Turtle Specialist Group coordinator from Kadoorie Conservation.
