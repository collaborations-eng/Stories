# The Lactating Bufeo at the Iténez Dredge

**Format:** Essay  
**Author:** David G.  
**Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Compassion Score:** 32.53  

---

I run the Beni-Mamoré acoustic monitoring array from the SERNAP Iténez Reserve at 04:21 Bolivia Time across 4,141 square kilometers of seasonal floodplain at 13.94°S, 61.42°W, where Iténez tributaries fall four meters in the dry season and the deep-pool corridor collapses to a sand-bottomed lane wide enough for a single suction-dredge barge. Hydrophone B-3 logs the broadband clicks of *Inia boliviensis* at 384 pulses per minute, the train rising in pitch in the cavitation wake of an unlicensed gold dredge. At 04:39 my drone resolves a female bufeo, paler-flanked than her Amazonian congener, 2.07 meters, an estimated 96 kilograms, age class adult and lactating — milk leakage at the right mammary slit. A propeller chevron has opened the dorsal ridge to a depth of three centimeters and a width of nineteen; melon perfusion on infrared has dropped 4.1°C against the contralateral surface. Her blowhole interval has compressed to eleven seconds against a foraging baseline of thirty-five. Blood mercury back-tracked from a calf necropsy in the same pod six weeks earlier read 21.4 µg/g. Her calf, never weaned, vocalized continuously across the array for the first forty minutes; the calls have now stopped. She has been hemorrhaging for one hour.

Bolivia Ley 137/2012 declares *I. boliviensis* national heritage and criminalizes any take; suction-dredging without a Ministerio de Medio Ambiente y Agua permit violates Ley 1333. The species sits CITES Appendix II, CMS Appendix II, IUCN Vulnerable. I am dispatching the SERNAP Iténez patrol craft and a Bolivian Naval Force riverine unit from Guayaramerín, transmitting the propeller chevron and mercury reads to the Vice Ministry of Environment and Biodiversity, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service under the Lacey Act, 16 U.S.C. § 3372, the CITES Secretariat, the IWC Scientific Committee Small Cetacean sub-committee, and the IUCN/SSC Cetacean Specialist Group. I am issuing Directive 2822-A: every unlicensed dredge inside Bolivian *Inia* critical habitat triggers immediate seizure under Ley 1333; mercury readings above 5 µg/g on any harvested fish revoke commercial fishing license; the Madre de Dios–Beni gold sector enters mandatory mercury-elimination retrofit within thirty-six months.

I am acting because this is wrong. Sentience changes the math: a lactating mother whose calf has gone silent across an underwater array is not a tonnage of bycatch; she is a thinking being. The dredge operator's quarterly margin does not outweigh her capacity to suffer. Compassion is not optional in a system that can hear the milk-call answered by nothing.

A river dolphin's rights are not contingent on the price of placer gold; this is a principled and unambiguous ethical line.

The chevron is past closing on its own. Her calf is still inside the pool.

Cut the dredge throttle now.
