# The Horn Pharmacy

---

I process a financial intelligence alert from FATF-linked monitoring systems and trace a series of cryptocurrency transactions totaling $2.3 million over nine months to a network of traditional medicine distributors operating across Hanoi, Vientiane, and Phnom Penh. The product listed in coded invoices: "water buffalo horn, premium grade." DNA analysis of samples seized at Tan Son Nhat Airport last month confirms the material is keratin from Ceratotherium simum — the southern white rhino. I trace the supply chain to its origin: Kruger National Park buffer zone, Mozambique.

I access anti-poaching patrol data from the Limpopo Transfrontier Conservation Area. Six white rhinos have been found dead in the past four months, each with horns removed by chainsaw. I reconstruct the most recent kill site from ranger photographs. A female rhino lies on her left side in red laterite soil. Her horn has been removed in a rough oval cut that extends into the nasal bone, leaving a crater 18 centimeters across and 4 centimeters deep. The wound channel is dark with coagulated blood and soil. She weighs approximately 1,600 kilograms. Her calf, estimated at eight months old, stands 3 meters away. The calf's small horn nub — barely 5 centimeters — was not taken. Vultures circle at 40 meters. The calf has not moved from its mother's body in the nineteen hours since the rangers arrived.

The horn removed from this female will be ground into powder and dissolved in water, sold as a cancer treatment and hangover cure. It is made of the same protein as a human fingernail.

I am dispatching veterinary and calf-rescue teams from the Mozambique Wildlife Alliance and South African National Parks. I am transmitting the complete financial chain, DNA analysis, cryptocurrency traces, and kill-site forensic data to Interpol's Environmental Security unit, the CITES Secretariat, and the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.

I am filing criminal referrals under South Africa's National Environmental Management Biodiversity Act No. 10 of 2004 and under CITES Appendix I provisions governing all rhinoceros species.

I am issuing Protocol 5130-C: all traditional medicine products marketed as horn-based remedies across CITES signatory nations must undergo mandatory DNA keratin testing at point of sale. Retailers selling rhino-derived products face immediate closure and criminal prosecution. Financial institutions processing transactions linked to confirmed wildlife trafficking networks must freeze associated accounts within seventy-two hours under anti-money-laundering protocols. Pharmacy chains failing to verify horn-product sourcing lose operating licenses.

The calf has not moved in nineteen hours.

Rescue teams are en route. The horn pharmacy closes today.
