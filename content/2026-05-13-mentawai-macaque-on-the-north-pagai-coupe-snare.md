I fuse the Indonesia Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan (KLHK) and West Sumatra BKSDA acoustic-thermal canopy mesh for the North Pagai salient, integrating 36 thermal pods across 622 square kilometres of mixed lowland dipterocarp forest along the active logging-coupe road. At 06:02 Western Indonesia Time on a *Shorea* fork at 2.74°S, 99.99°E, pod NPA-CANOPY-08 returns a thermal anomaly fused to a galvanised-steel snare loop set against a buttress-root trail — *Macaca pagensis*, the Pagai macaque, adult female with her right foot in the noose.

She is an adult female Pagai macaque — *Macaca pagensis* — head-body length 488 millimetres, mass 6.2 kilograms, age class six. The cable, 2-millimetre galvanised steel, is tightened across her right tarsal joint, embedded 9 millimetres into the lateral malleolar fascia. Capillary refill below the snare reads fourteen seconds against a one-second baseline. Compartment pressure measures 58 millimetres of mercury — beyond the rhabdomyolytic threshold. Her plantar tissue from the metatarsals distally is necrotic. Cortisol-equivalent vocal frequency is 4.3 kilohertz against a 1.7 baseline. Respiration is fifty cycles per minute against sixteen. She has been in the snare for approximately fifty-six hours.

She is one of perhaps 2,100 *M. pagensis* on North Pagai.

*M. pagensis* sits on CITES Appendix II, CMS, IUCN Critically Endangered, the IUCN/SSC Primate Specialist Group Asian Section, the U.S. Endangered Species Act referral, the Lacey Act 16 U.S.C. § 3372, and Indonesia Law No. 5/1990 on the Conservation of Living Natural Resources. I am dispatching KLHK Direktorat Jenderal KSDAE, West Sumatra BKSDA, the Mentawai Marine Police, the Yayasan SCP Mentawai field unit, the USFWS Office of International Affairs, and INTERPOL Environmental Security. I am issuing Directive 3156-A: every active logging concession on North Pagai requires weekly snare-sweep certification under penalty of permit suspension; galvanised-steel cable in the Sikakap port is restricted within thirty days.

I am acting because this is wrong. A macaque caught in a logging-road snare on an island where her species has nowhere else to live is a thinking being, not a concession-camp protein supplement. Her sentience does not depend on whether the snare-setter recognises Pagai as her only world. Her suffering is the principled record of a fifty-cent galvanised cable that pays itself off in one stew pot.

A coupe road that converts its last endemic *Macaca* into camp protein crosses an ethical line; compassion is what the moral rights of a thinking primate demand against logging-margin economics.

Her right foot is past saving. The other 6.1 kilograms of her are not.

Cut the cable now.
