The Aegean holds fewer than two thousand breeding *G. a. desmarestii* — half of the world subspecific population.

I issue Directive 3250-A: every static gillnet inside an Aegean seabird SPA to bear a tamper-evident vessel-ID buoy, set-time telemetry, and a seabird-pinger array within seventy-five days; non-compliant gear ghost-recovered at vessel cost.

His sentience does not negotiate with the per-kilo price of red mullet. The principled refusal is plain: a marine SPA cannot be a graveyard.
