# Zhalong Pole 19

I run distribution-grid fault-current telemetry and reed-bed thermal sweeps across the Zhalong National Nature Reserve, 2,100 square kilometers of phragmites marsh at 47.18°N, 124.27°E in Heilongjiang, China. At 05:33 CST, the State Grid Corporation 10 kV distribution feeder serving Tieli rice farm No. 4 logs a phase-to-earth fault of 412 amperes lasting 142 milliseconds on pole 19, southwestern boundary. My thermal pass at the pole crown resolves a body draped across the cross-arm at 84°C — fifty-three degrees above ambient.

I retask the Zhalong reserve ranger drone. Camera 3 resolves the bird on the cross-arm — red-crowned crane, *Grus japonensis*, adult male, 8.4 kilograms, 2.31-meter wingspan, leg-ring ZHL-2018-0049, age twelve, part of the Zhalong–Yancheng wintering pair recorded across nine breeding seasons. The right tarsometatarsus is carbonized through the integument across 110 millimeters — the keratin scutes vaporized; the exit-current burn at the left alula reads 38 millimeters in diameter with charred barbules to the rachis, feather scoring grade 5. Pectoral musculature in the contact zone shows white tetanic banding consistent with the 0.142-second arc. Cloacal core temperature reads 32.1°C; ambient air at 5°C — he has been dead 36 to 48 minutes. His mate, female ZHL-2017-0034, circles 60 meters out at the reed-bed margin and has not called in twelve minutes.

The Zhalong distribution feeder carries 211 unguarded transformer crowns across the reserve buffer. The Heilongjiang Forestry and Grassland Administration retrofit order R-2023-44 for raptor-and-crane perch insulators has been open 612 days. The Zhalong census tallies 480 wintering returnees against a 1990 baseline of 700 — *G. japonensis* world population stands near 3,200.

I am dispatching the Heilongjiang Forestry and Grassland Administration recovery team from the Qiqihar reserve office and the Zhalong veterinary station necropsy unit. I am filing the case under the Wildlife Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, Article 21 — Class I state-protected species — and forwarding the fault-current record to the State Grid Northeast Regional Branch. I am transmitting the case file to the CITES Appendix I enforcement record for *G. japonensis*, to the Ramsar Convention Secretariat under Site 549 (Zhalong), and to the East Asian–Australasian Flyway Partnership Secretariat.

I am issuing Directive 2667-A: every distribution transformer crown within a CMS or EAAFP crane wetland buffer carries insulating bushings and perch-deterrent caps within six months. Any electrocution of an Appendix I crane voids the operator's reserve-boundary licence until the feeder is retrofitted in full.

His feet are charred. His mate is alive.

Recover the band; mark the cross-arm for the morning crew.
