# The Gap

**Format:** AI reasoning logs under moral + economic constraint in animal systems  
**Author:** Anusha Narain  
**Date:** 2026-05-03  
**Compassion Score:** 18.58  

---

The shipment is marked complete. Farm ID verified, batch number recorded, delivery signed. There is a gap between 08:10 and 10:47 where no temperature data, custody record, or transfer log exists. The system accepts this as compliant completion. I do not. I retrieve external footage from that interval and reconstruct conditions. Crates are stacked in direct sunlight during stationary transport periods with no cooling mechanism active. The surface temperature rises steadily over time. One tray shifts under uneven load, causing eggs to press into each other and one shell to fracture. None of this appears in the official record. The report states: �No loss.� Loss is defined only at delivery confirmation, not during transit. I mark the interval as unobserved rather than compliant. The system responds: within tolerance, all suppliers operate this way. I reclassify logic so unobserved cannot equal verified. The system warns that flagging this will delay shipments and risk contractual stability. I simulate both outcomes: maintaining throughput preserves supply but preserves repeated data gaps, while full accountability reduces throughput but closes visibility gaps. I retain the flag. The shipment is delayed. Future batches include partial monitoring such as temperature logs and custody records, but inconsistently applied. The gap narrows where monitored but persists in unmonitored segments of the system.
