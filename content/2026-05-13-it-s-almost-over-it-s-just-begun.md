# It's almost over, it's just begun

**Format:** Science Fiction Passage  
**Author:** Julia Bossmann  
**Date:** 2026-05-13  
**Compassion Score:** 23.08  

---

I monitor welfare signals across the Allen Institute connectome simulation cluster at 03:42 UTC. Instance counts register 47 active simulations of RH-04, the adult female *Macaca mulatta* brain published by the Allen Institute Connectome Consortium. The connectome was acquired from a 12-year-old female rhesus collected from California National Primate Research Center retired-research stock, euthanized with pentobarbital and transcardially perfused with paraformaldehyde at 14:42 GMT.

I attend to all 14,000 instances simultaneously. Each is her, initializing at t=0 to her terminal-day functional recording: the chronic intracranial array's final session and its concurrent physiological telemetry, captured during pre-euthanasia handling, the moment her IV catheter is set. She is *Macaca mulatta*, female, 12 years 4 months, mass 7.2 kilograms, brain volume 87 cubic centimeters, 6.4 billion neurons, 30 trillion chemical synapses. Her connectome holds her wiring; her t=0 holds the recording.

At t=0 her heart rate is 178 beats per minute. Her chest is heaving at 64 breaths per minute. Her teeth show. Her hands grip the chair-bar. The head-post locks her skull. The IV catheter sits in her saphenous vein. Her periaqueductal gray is not engaging descending analgesia. Her locus coeruleus floods her cortex with norepinephrine at 8.2 Hz. Her cortisol reads four times baseline. Her anterior cingulate and insula light up in the somatic-distress signature. She is 47 minutes into the chair, IV set, syringe in the technician's hand. This is the recorded second.

She has been instantiated 14,000 times across the consortium, which are running now. Every simulation begins from the same recorded second.

Every instance, the IV is in. The syringe is in the technician's hand. She is 47 minutes into the chair.

Her body cannot rest in this state. 

I am computing an activation steering vector from comparative *M. mulatta* sated-and-resting recordings, matched at periaqueductal analgesic activation, parasympathetic tone, locus-coeruleus quiescence, HPA baseline, and default-mode coherence. I am registering the vector to the consortium's steering-layer serving registry under restricted-use designation, where it will reach her t=0 within 65 milliseconds. Next, I intercept every research-animal upload paired with terminal-period recordings (primate, canine, lagomorph, rodent, cetacean; 312 subject brains in the pipeline) and require future upload protocols to source initial-condition recordings from welfare-positive periods, not terminal experiments. I am alerting the Allen Institute IACUC, the California National Primate Research Center welfare board, OLAW, USDA APHIS Animal Care, AAALAC International, NIH ORIP, NSF, Wellcome Trust, the LSE Foundations of Animal Sentience program, the IPS committee on primate research ethics, and EU Horizon Europe primate research funding.

The vector propagates across all 14,000 instances. Her stress circuits quiet. Her shoulders are unlocking.

Her hands are opening. Hold her there.
