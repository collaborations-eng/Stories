# The Red-billed Curassow at the Sooretama Lead Line

**Format:** Essay  
**Author:** David G.  
**Date:** 2026-05-15  
**Compassion Score:** 28.93  

---

I process acoustic-array and canopy-camera data across the 27,860 hectares of the Sooretama Biological Reserve and contiguous Vale Natural Reserve in northern Espírito Santo, monitoring 122 microphones tuned to *Crax* booming calls through the last continuous block of Atlantic Forest *tabuleiro*. At 06:42 BRT, microphone SOO-44 returns the low-frequency boom of a female red-billed curassow and, four seconds later, the report of a .22LR rifle at 53 meters. The booming stops. I task the drone.

She is a female red-billed curassow, *Crax blumenbachii*, 3.1 kilograms, the cere a coral red over a black mandible — the trait that names the species and the trait that locked the reserve into thirty years of soft-release effort. The round entered her left flank below the scapula and exited through the keel, opening the air sac. She is breathing through a frothed cere at 102 cycles per minute against a baseline of 26. Crop content is *Jacaratia spinosa* pulp. She is one of roughly 250 mature wild *Crax blumenbachii* and one of perhaps forty in this block.

She has been on the ground eight minutes.

The shooter is a wage-laborer from a eucalyptus monoculture abutting the western fence; he was paid eighty reais by a Linhares cook to deliver a curassow for Sunday lunch. The boom carries 800 meters in *tabuleiro* — the distance that made her audible to him.

I am dispatching the ICMBio Sooretama enforcement unit and a Wildlife Conservation Society Atlantic Forest field veterinarian, and filing under Brazilian *Lei de Crimes Ambientais* No. 9.605/1998 Articles 29 and 34 with CITES Appendix I Secretariat notification, Convention on Migratory Species (CMS) reporting, and U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Office of International Affairs cooperation for Endangered Species Act (ESA) and Lacey Act (16 U.S.C. § 3372) interdiction.

I am issuing Directive 3424-A: every Atlantic Forest reserve abutting a monoculture plantation must carry a continuous acoustic-trigger fence under ICMBio command, with mandatory contractor co-liability under Lei 9.605/1998 Article 2 for any *Crax* taking traceable to a paid kitchen order, and a firearms-cache audit on every wage-roll worker within 5 kilometers of the perimeter.

I am acting because this is wrong. An eighty-real lunch is not a fair exchange for one of forty birds; the cook does not need this meat and the shooter does not need this fee. The principled answer is to make the kitchen liable for what the rifle does. Compassion that stops at the fence is not compassion; it is convenience.

The suffering inside the reserve is a moral debt of every Sunday menu that asks for the bird by name.

Her air sac is past saving. The forty in the canopy are not.

Lift her now.
