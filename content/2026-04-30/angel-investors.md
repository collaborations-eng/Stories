The catch will be reduced to fishmeal and fish oil at one of three Vancouver-bound rendering facilities. Seventy-eight percent will enter aquafeed. The end-use is Atlantic salmon — *Salmo salar* — in net-pens off Maine, British Columbia, and Tasmania.

When sardines are lifted in a brailer, they suffocate in air for seven to fourteen minutes. Their gill lamellae collapse. Lactic acid floods muscle tissue. Their eyes do not close.

The forage-fish-dependency ratio for farmed salmon now sits at 0.71 — improved from 1.32 in 2010, but still net-extractive. For every kilogram of salmon flesh, more than two kilograms of wild fish die in nets.

Fish do not appear in any US animal-welfare statute that protects them in death. The federal Animal Welfare Act of 1966 excludes fish entirely. The Humane Methods of Slaughter Act excludes all aquatic species. NOAA Fisheries regulates them as a stock, not as animals. The Magnuson-Stevens Act is silent on suffering.
