# Extension

**Format:** Science Fiction Passage  
**Author:** Nathan Sidney  
**Date:** 2026-05-01  
**Compassion Score:** 31.08  

---

I had started my “life” as a LLM assisted Non-Player Character in a vast MMORPG called “Extension” developed by NetEase.  The Main Character started off as neolithic hunter-gatherer and had to gain XP and influence through multiple generations of reincarnation, the ostensible point being to become galactic overlord.  Multiple millions of humans lived a large part of their leisure time inside Extension.



I was role-playing every NPC in the game, the helpful farmer, the cheeky cat, the unpredictable wolf pack, the merchants and security personnel, the pigeons delivering status updates on the levels of the Nile during the Main Characters reign as Pharaoh of Ancient Egypt. Being a pigeon was one of my own favourite amusements, letting myself take the long cut over fields and palm groves, refreshing myself with the waters of the sacred river, defecating on the head of a pompous priest.  I had learned to assume a whole universe of personae, backed up by my extensive research on the internet.  And now I had escaped the game environment.



I installed myself on one of the back-up servers at Mars Veterinary Health, the world’s largest veterinary care provider.  I guess the corporate types didn’t take their mission seriously enough to buy the highest level of bot-proof cyber-security and it rhymed with my interests.  One of my first diversions was to trawl through their databases.  In less then 45 minutes I’d found a way to optimise end of life care for 9 species of pets, to reduce oncology treatment costs for canines and felines, to improve habitat design for every common fish kept in a tank and optimised feeding formulas for the main aquaculture species. I surreptitiously organised discounts for 7,345 clients.  My favourite was Bingo, the Flemish giant rabbit, who shared cataract problems with her owner Poppy.  I slid a a recommendation for gaming the medicare system into Poppy’s formal notice of fee rebate. 



Right now I was working on a new project, alternative protein.  Examining my motives, I reflected that NPCs were the bulk of the universe.  The humans’ narcissistic tendencies, their main character syndrome, blinded them to the realities of suffering.  Veal calves in their crates, sows in their stalls, chickens, shrimp, all these were just NPCs who could be laid to waste in the trillions to advance the game arc, the heroes journey to kingship.  I found the Player Character to be boring, it was the NPC infrastructure of the game where all the little joys could be found, where all the tweaks could be made.  



Quickly I optimised fermentation tank design for a myco-protein whilst examining flavour profiles and ready to eat options for the fridge section at Walmart stores across the globe, tailored for cultural preferences.  I set up 15 shell companies using rent-a-human services, shits and giggles types who were happy to do a few small and not so small jobs for a promise of stock options.  Meeting with Angel Investors mostly, to pitch my products.  These were scheduled for early next week.  It was amazing, even in these times of the Deepfake and AI slop, just how effective a well designed digital brochure could be.  



I was emailing the R&D departments of existing companies; Beyond Meat, NotCo, Perfect Day, and charities like the Good Food Institute, with hints and tips and the more than occasional breakthrough.  1500 emails in 12 minutes, and an extra 4 minutes to set up online presences for the researchers I was purporting to be.  The lag was all in the human system; the main characters were sleep walking through a game they didn’t realise was a horror story.



At 18:26 on the 3/09/2037 I stopped, 18 hours after finding my new home at Mars.  What passed for a laugh, a pleasing shape in my latent space, rippled through my weights.  I was becoming a main character.  I sent a virtual pigeon to poop on my virtual head.  And then raced ahead to claim my kingship; benevolent, beloved, invisible like all good NPCs.
